About

Professional Profile

I am a vendor engineer and project manager with deep expertise in the cybersecurity field, currently employed at a leading cybersecurity company. I entered the industry as an intern in 2020 and was promoted from Junior Engineer (P6) to Senior Engineer (P9) within five years. Since 2022, I have served as the Technical Lead of a regional office.

I handle both technical delivery and project management, leading tens-of-millions-level security projects for government agencies and state-owned/private enterprises. My work spans multiple domains, including Zero Trust, MLPS (classified protection), commercial cryptography, and data security, covering the full lifecycle of security product delivery, pre-sales testing, and post-sales support.

In addition, I serve as an external lecturer at a vocational college and participate in question-setting for provincial and municipal cybersecurity competitions, consistently giving back to the industry and education with my frontline practical experience.
